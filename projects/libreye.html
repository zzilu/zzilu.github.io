Libreye — Modular Security Camera Enclosure

Mechanical design, Creo Simulate FEA, and FDM PLA prototyping for a compact, cloud-free security camera built on a 3-person team for the Foothill Innovation Challenge (May – Jun. 2026).

01 · Hardware & Internal Packaging

Enclosure Layout & Early Prototype

I was the sole mechanical engineer on a 3-person team (software, hardware, mechanical) and owned the CAD, 3D printing, and assembly of the PLA housing.

  • Internal hardware consists of two stacked 3000 mAh 3.7 V LiPo batteries connected to a boost converter (mounted to the right of the batteries, directly above the power switch), which then wires into the main camera PCB.
  • Modeled in Creo Parametric using top-down design, surfacing, and parametric relations so mounting bosses, port cutouts, and internal clearances updated across FDM PLA print iterations.
02 · Structural Analysis

Creo Simulate FEA — Before vs. After Stiffening

Tested the main shell in Creo Simulate under a 250 N side load with the mating face fixed. The baseline wall deflected 0.9752 mm and concentrated stress at the side port cutouts. Adding internal ribs and root fillets cut max deflection to 0.5846 mm (+67% stiffness) and reduced peak von Mises stress from 26.04 MPa to 22.77 MPa (-12%) while adding 0.8 g (+2.9%) of mass.
