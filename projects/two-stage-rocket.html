Why the Avionics Bay Was Re-Architected

Mk1 → Mk2 Redesign

New electronics were added mid-project, and miscommunication between the avionics/electronics team and the structures/design team meant the original Mk1 sled did not have enough room. I redesigned the bay into the Mk2 configuration with two parallel vertical mounts to create more surface area for the additional boards.
